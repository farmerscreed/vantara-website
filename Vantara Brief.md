\#\# 1\. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Build a single-page (with anchored sections) corporate website for \*\*Vantara International Limited\*\*, an international property development company headquartered in Port Harcourt, Nigeria. The company's flagship project is \*\*Primerose Smart City Cluster\*\* — a 200-building, 800-unit smart residential development within New Port City, a 1,000-hectare government-backed smart city in Eleme, Rivers State, Nigeria.

\*\*Primary objective:\*\* Project credibility, authority, and scale. The site must immediately communicate that Vantara is a serious, well-capitalized international development company — not a small local builder. The audience includes international manufacturers (particularly Chinese suppliers at trade fairs), potential investors, and prospective buyers.

\*\*Secondary objective:\*\* Light-touch lead capture through partnership and contact inquiry forms.

\#\#\# 2.2 Typography

Use premium, distinctive typefaces. Recommended pairings (or equivalents):

\- \*\*Display / Headings:\*\* A refined serif or architectural sans — consider \*Cormorant Garamond\*, \*Playfair Display\*, or \*Libre Baskerville\* for editorial luxury weight.  
\- \*\*Body / UI:\*\* A clean geometric sans — \*DM Sans\*, \*Outfit\*, or \*General Sans\* (avoid Inter, Roboto, Arial).  
\- \*\*Accent / Stats:\*\* A condensed uppercase sans for large stat numbers — \*Bebas Neue\*, \*Oswald\*, or \*Big Shoulders Display\*.

\#\# 4\. TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

\#\#\# 4.1 Performance & SEO

\- \*\*SSR or static-generated\*\* — use Next.js (preferred), Astro, or equivalent. Not a pure SPA — must be crawlable.  
\- \*\*Meta tags:\*\* Title: "Vantara International — Smart Cities. Sustainable Living. Global Vision." Description: "Vantara International is a property development company building Primerose Smart City Cluster — 200 smart buildings within New Port City, Rivers State, Nigeria."  
\- \*\*Open Graph tags\*\* with a social share image (\`OG\_IMAGE.jpg\` — the client's best render at 1200×630px).  
\- \*\*Structured data:\*\* Organization schema (JSON-LD) with name, url, logo, contact.  
\- \*\*Sitemap.xml\*\* and \*\*robots.txt\*\* — standard, allow all crawlers.  
\- \*\*Page speed:\*\* Target 90+ on Lighthouse. Compress video (H.264, max 8MB for hero loop). Lazy-load render images. Use WebP with JPEG fallback.

\#\#\# 4.2 Responsive Design

\- \*\*Desktop-first design\*\* but fully responsive.  
\- Hero video swaps to a static image (\`HERO\_STILL.jpg\` — a single frame from the video) on mobile to save bandwidth.  
\- Navigation collapses to hamburger menu on mobile.  
\- Stats bar stacks to 2×3 grid on mobile.  
\- Render gallery becomes a swipeable carousel on mobile.

\#\#\# 4.3 Hosting

\- \*\*Recommended:\*\* Vercel (if Next.js) or Netlify. Fast global CDN, easy DNS setup.  
\- \*\*Domain:\*\* vantarainternational.com — client has DNS control.  
\- \*\*SSL:\*\* Mandatory. Auto-provisioned via hosting platform.
